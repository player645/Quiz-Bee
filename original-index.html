How it works

You will answer 40 questions, one at a time, with no timer. Answers are checked right away and the correct answer is always shown.

  • Section One: 20 multiple-choice questions, 1 point each (20 points).
  • Section Two: 20 identification questions, typed answers, 2 points each (40 points).
  • Maximum score: 60 points.
